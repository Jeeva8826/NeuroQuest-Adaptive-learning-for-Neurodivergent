* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Hello everyone. We are Team ZEUEZ502, and this is NeuroQuest, our solution for Smart Education. The core idea is simple: the learning objective can remain the same, while the learning experience adapts to the learner."*

#### [0:15 – 0:55] Slide 2: Problem, Solution & Uniqueness
* **Display:** Slide 2 (Problem Statement, How Does It Solve the Problem?, Uniqueness).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Traditional digital learning platforms often present the same information, navigation, and rigid interaction pattern to everyone. But learners experience very different barriers—excessive information, confusing organization, sensory distraction, rigid pacing, or anxiety when asking for help. NeuroQuest treats these not as reasons to label a learner, but as signals for adapting the learning experience.*
  > 
  > *Our platform combines an adaptive interface, non-diagnostic learner modeling, behavioral evidence, controlled adaptation, and contextual AI assistance. Crucially, NeuroQuest never diagnoses the learner. It works with learning preferences, support accommodations, and observable interaction signals, keeping the student in control while keeping the NCERT learning objective intact."*

#### [0:55 – 1:35] Slide 3: Technical Approach & Closed Feedback Loop
* **Display:** Slide 3 (Technical Approach, Tech Stack & Feedback Loop diagram).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"Technically, NeuroQuest operates as a closed adaptive learning loop. Learner interactions—such as response pacing, navigation velocity, and hint requests—stream into a behavioral feature processing pipeline.*
  > 
  > *Our Scikit-learn cognitive state classifier and Bayesian Knowledge Tracing engine analyze learner patterns in real time. The Adaptive Intelligence Engine then dynamically adjusts task density, presentation modality, and graduated scaffolding—updating the learner's profile for the next session without ever storing clinical deficit tags."*

#### [1:35 – 2:05] Slide 4: Feasibility & Viability
* **Display:** Slide 4 (Feasibility Analysis, Economic, Operational, Market).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"NeuroQuest is architecturally and economically viable. By utilizing client-side browser APIs—including Web Speech and local eye-gaze telemetry—alongside deterministic 384-dimensional dense semantic vector retrieval via Neon Serverless PGVector, our system operates with zero mandatory per-token API fees.*
  > 
  > *It seamlessly integrates with LMS platforms like Canvas and Moodle via standard LTI, and adheres strictly to WCAG 2.1 AA, Universal Design for Learning, and the RPwD Act 2016."*

#### [2:05 – 2:25] Slide 5: Impact & Benefits
* **Display:** Slide 5 (Impacts & Benefits for Learners, Educators, EdTech).
* **Voiceover:**
  > *"For learners, NeuroQuest provides real-time, non-punitive guidance that maintains flow state through self-paced mastery trees. For educators and caregivers, it provides transparent behavioral insights without medical stigma. For EdTech platforms, our modular API enables scalable personalized learning for millions of students across NCERT Standards 1 to 10."*

#### [2:25 – 2:30] Slide 6: Research Grounding & Live Transition
* **Display:** Slide 6 (Research and References).
* **Voiceover:**
